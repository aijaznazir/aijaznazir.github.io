I am Aijaz Nazir, a PhD student in Mathematics at the University of Houston. My interests sit at the intersection of numerical analysis, partial differential equations, dynamical systems, and modern machine learning.

I am especially interested in using mathematical structure — conservation laws, invariants, operators, and geometry — to design learning methods that are reliable and interpretable.
